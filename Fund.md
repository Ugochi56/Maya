# Fundamental Analysis for my stock investing bot



1. understand what i am buying
2. dive into the financials (revenue growth, profit growth, profit margin, and debt)
3. work out if the business has an advantage (the moat... impossible patterns to replicate)
4. look at the management
5. check the company valuation
6. look at the P/E ratio (context needed)
7. price-to-sales
8. price-to-free-cash-flow
9. enterprise-value-to-EBITDA



## How to read a stock

* Become a customer
* Listen to the customers reviews
* Watch what the company actually does
* Study the competition
* Study the money
